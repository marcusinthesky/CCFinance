# Company mention annotation

Read the frozen passage as written. Label every explicit occurrence of a named
business, including private firms, named subsidiaries and business names used in
publication or attribution. A short name counts when it identifies an operating
business in context. Exclude bare stock symbols, exchange codes, government
bodies, unions, universities and other nonbusiness organizations. Exclude named
products, indices and publications when the wording refers only to the product,
index or publication. A named venue counts when it refers to the exchange
operator as a business. A standalone URL and navigation or app product labels
do not establish a company mention.

Mark each occurrence independently. Retain legal suffixes, their punctuation
and internal punctuation when part of the name. Exclude possessive markers,
external punctuation, determiners outside the name and descriptive modifiers.
Record half-open Unicode code-point offsets from the unchanged passage start.
Keep passages without mentions. For a cut-off name, label only when the visible
text independently identifies a company.

Two independent Codex agents receive the passage text and this guide. The
primary pass covers all 40 leads. The repeat pass covers the first two frozen
ranks per publication year and receives neither primary labels nor predictions.
Resolve disagreements from the passage and the guide before viewing either
extractor's output. Retain both annotation passes and the adjudication record.
