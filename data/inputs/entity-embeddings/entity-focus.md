# Entity-focused document embeddings

The unit of analysis is a story containing two linked issuers. Encode the same body twice, switching the focal issuer. For each vector, compare cosine similarity to the two independently generated issuer accounts in the same encoder space. A story succeeds only if both vectors strictly prefer their own account. A tied or unchanged vector cannot pass this test.

The frozen sample contains 250 story clusters across ten year-by-roster strata. Each stratum contributes ten development and fifteen confirmation stories. Five of the latter receive an independent Terra assessment. Luna supplies a named account, a masked account and a verbatim supporting quotation per supported issuer. The pair judge determines whether the accounts are distinguishable. The primary analysis uses masked accounts and full-length stories that fit every encoder. Report coverage alongside accuracy because incidental mentions, indistinguishable events and context limits can change the eligible population.

For OpenSearch neural embed v1, Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B and EmbeddingGemma-300M, select among the focal-company header, task instruction, inline mention markers and query-style instruction on development stories. Break accuracy ties by mean margin, then declaration order. Compare the frozen winner with a simple focal-company header on confirmation. Controls include the unchanged body, company name alone and sentences mentioning the issuer. Gemma also tests adding the headline. Named references and native-length scores are secondary.

A model qualifies with at least 60 eligible Luna stories, at least 20 Terra audit stories, a Luna gain of at least ten percentage points over the header with a positive lower paired 95% bootstrap bound, and a positive Terra gain. Declare a unique model winner only if its paired lower bounds against both alternatives exceed zero after adjustment for three comparisons. These thresholds screen for a follow-up study. They do not establish performance on cross-story retrieval or single-issuer news.

Run `devenv shell -- just dvc repro entity_focus_test_score` from the repository root. The scoring stage exports coverage, article-level margins, comparison estimates and the decision locally. Each model's Parquet shards retain token counts, clipping flags, dropped mentions and whether clipping removed a Luna supporting quotation.
