## Replication evidence for the main text

- **Baker, Bloom, Davis and Kost, equity market volatility.** Use the authors’ July 2025 manuscript §2.1 and Table 1 and its 5 August 2025 Mendeley archive v1. The subsequent paper is in *Journal of Financial Economics* 175 (2026), 104187. Six monthly fits reproduce the archive figures at displayed precision. For the corpus comparison, four hosts contribute 675,234 eligible canonical articles over 49 common months (September 2019–September 2023), with one host shared with the authors’ eleven-newspaper archive. Build the standardized dictionary-hit shares by publisher-month. With an intercept and contemporaneous tracker on shared FRED-based VIX, the preferred comparison is CCFinance R² 0.515 versus authors’ 0.647, difference −0.132 [−0.215, +0.110] from a paired six-month block bootstrap. This is contemporaneous fit on one short calendar and conditions on the trackers. March–April 2020 influences the gap, and point estimates may reflect source and calibration differences. Append realized-volatility results and other specifications. - **Kazinnik, Killen, Scidá and Wu, bank networks.** Use the 6 July 2023 CEBRA manuscript Tables 1–2 and Figures 2–4 for roster and exact comparison. The 2026 *Journal of Banking & Finance* article is 187, 107694. Its preview confirms the design, with detailed final tables and Factiva tags unavailable in this audit. Across 13–19 January and 13–19 April 2020, count each unordered pair once per eligible article after issuer-to-bank mapping. Of 34 reference nodes, 33 have mapped issuers, including foreign-parent proxies. The preferred result is 177 to 242 distinct connected pairs in CCFinance versus 210 to 172 in the CEBRA manuscript. - **López-Lira and Tang, overnight drift.** Use arXiv:2304.07619v6 (28 October 2025), §§4, 7.4 and Table 8, and the 15 June 2026 Mendeley archive v2 for the checked protocol. The extra first-publication parser raises both-leg days to 110 and yields −7.73 bp over 340 evaluated days after refitting. ## Further work, in priority order

- **Highest value for a comparative NER claim.** Freeze a fresh article sample independently of extracted spans, with human markup of organization boundaries, issuer identity or unresolved status, and article focus. Double-label a subset and adjudicate disagreements. Define treatment of subsidiaries, brands, nonlisted organizations and overlapping names before scoring. Run the pinned GLiNER Small, Stanza and CoreNLP on identical text. Report exact-span precision, recall and F1, then dated issuer-pair precision and recall using one fixed linker. Include a gold-span linker run to localize error. Resample at the story level and report uncertainty and support. A FiNER-ORD test-split comparison provides a complementary external check.
- **Highest value for the corpus selection claim.** Estimate article-level financial relevance inside the hostname filter and inspect a probability sample outside it for missed financial stories. Predefine strata by year and publisher type, keep inclusion probabilities and compare overlap of canonical stories across sources. Increase cluster draws to a prespecified precision only if claiming that one upstream source offers better coverage. Without this study, describe the selection as a publisher filter.
- **Highest value for a historical issuer claim.** Inspect false links and abstentions around renamings, ticker reuse, parent–subsidiary links and brand ownership. Compare to an exact dated-name/ticker baseline. For a claim about contemporaneously available mapping, rerun learned alias priors using only earlier anchors; date-valid candidates alone do not ensure time-valid learned priors.
- **Conditional on a firm-vector performance claim.** Freeze header, instruction and issuer-sentence arms; sample fresh multi-firm stories until the planned eligibility threshold is met or report the shortfall. Record shared-sentence masks, truncation and supporting-quote coverage. Preserve name masking and role-swap controls, use paired intervals, and keep deals together in cross-story tests. Retain the current result as exploratory if further confirmation is outside scope.
- **Paper assembly.** Carry the selected exhibits into step 03. In steps 04–06 introduce each sampling unit before interpreting it. In step 07 check every quantitative comparison against the exact paper version, table, denominator and label source. Identify human labels, model judgments and deterministic checks in captions.
