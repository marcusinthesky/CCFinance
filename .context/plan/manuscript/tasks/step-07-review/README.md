# 07. Review the argument and audit the evidence

## Goal

Read the draft as a new reader and check its empirical claims against the sources.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Read the abstract, section openings and endings, and conclusion in sequence. Record what the reader now knows at each handoff. Where a passage mainly anticipates criticism, lead with the supported observation and keep its qualification at the first inference it changes. Verify that restructuring preserves adverse results, uncertainty and release limits.
- [ ] Cold-read the technical pages alone. Write a short assessment from an ML reviewer and a finance reviewer, then an organizer-style synthesis of benchmark contribution, strongest evidence and unresolved concern. Link each concern to a passage and revision or a justified limitation.
- [ ] Reverse-outline the draft without consulting the scored outline. Record the passage, reader difficulty and smallest useful revision for each substantive issue.
- [ ] Trace reported numbers, denominators, sample dates and uncertainty back to their outputs. Check replication language, release claims and rights against the evidence map. Resolve findings or document outstanding limits.
