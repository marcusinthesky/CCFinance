| Finding | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Link precision and subject recovery have different reference units, and the stricter focus rule loses subject recall. | Choose a focus screen based on whether accepting incidental coverage or missing subject stories is more costly. Report which issuers pass it. |
| Press-release share and transaction recall show that labels can distort a constructed sample. | Corroborate form labels before estimating composition and avoid an unaudited event-only eligibility gate. |

| The wider bank calendar requires narrower common support and the connected-pair and weight draw ranges cross zero. | Report both matched designs, their changed support and descriptive ranges before interpreting a week-specific increase. |
| Baseline drift is compatible with zero, and the larger clock-recovered panel still has a negative point estimate. | Use clock recovery to widen evaluable support. Treat drift as a retrospective measurement with leg support, uncertainty and costs, without assigning the design difference to timing alone. |
