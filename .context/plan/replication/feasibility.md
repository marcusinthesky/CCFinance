# Replication feasibility evidence

These November 2026 measurements informed the work breakdown. Rebuild the
production ledgers from versioned inputs. Coverage and fitted effects can
change when the final mappings, samples and publication rules are applied.

## Baker

From September 2019 through September 2023, Benzinga, Forbes, MarketWatch and
The Wall Street Journal contributed 675,234 usable articles. A title-and-body
dictionary pass found 21,082 hits and at least one hit in each of the 196
publisher-month cells. The pilot accepted an extra Standard & Poor spelling.
Benzinga covered only five publication days in September 2023. These facts
support a 49-month candidate panel and a specific coverage sensitivity.
The final dictionary and covariance convention require source verification.
[Replication archive](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/rmhtcwnvrk/1)

## Kazinnik

A 12-bank feasibility subset yielded 1,378 multi-bank articles in January 2020
and 1,919 in April, contributing 4,408 and 6,570 unordered pair observations.
Counting each normalized headline once per month reduced the pair totals by
13.2% and 15.5%. Some article records reside in adjacent capture months.
The subset and headline sensitivity were explored before protocol finalization.
The final roster and grouping come from the published study.
[Article](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbankfin.2026.107694)

## Lopez-Lira and Tang

The pilot used frozen first-publication evidence from Business Standard and
Mint Lifestyle, all available linked issuers, the CCFinance body-focus rule and
the existing Gemma body vectors. After listing, repeat-text and price screens,
282 candidate issuers contributed 1,598 firm-sessions in the October 2021 to
May 2024 evaluation window. Public listing and repeat-text evidence only
approximate historical CRSP share codes and RavenPack event novelty.

The first four monthly training sets had 5, 41, 71 and 97 observations. The
100-row minimum admitted 28 monthly fits per target starting in February 2022.
Across 422 sessions with predictions, both legs were active on 74 reaction
days and 70 drift days. For each target, 26 fits warned at the ten-iteration
limit. These are implementation and coverage diagnostics. Acceptance depends
on reproducing the rules and explaining attrition, irrespective of effect size.
