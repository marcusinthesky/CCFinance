# Matched earnings-week bank networks

The January–April 2020 increase survives equalizing the number and publisher
mix of bank stories after collapsing syndications. Connected pairs rose by
56.3 on average and total pair weight rose by 307.6. Neither of the two usable
placebo periods had changes as large, in either the positive direction or
absolute magnitude. The comparison is descriptive with two placebo periods.

The original raw January and April results reproduce at 177 and 242 pairs,
with weights of 2,362 and 3,203. Collapsing stories preserves the two distinct
pair counts while reducing weights to 1,984 and 2,742. Exact publisher
matching retains 682 stories per week from 31 of the 71 representative hosts.
That keeps 43–53% of stories, depending on the week. Nasdaq.com accounts for
271 of the 682 matched stories. The result describes this publisher mix.

Matching equates the volume of bank-linked stories. The mix of subject matter,
prominence and article size can still differ within a host. An identical
headline can group unrelated versions, and the cluster and hash rules can miss
rewrites. A representative may omit entities named only in other copies.
The ranges use story subsampling and cover variability from selecting matched
stories. There are two week-level reference changes. July–October spans
different calendar quarters and both placebo periods also fall during the
pandemic. The historical rank therefore gives a limited reference for how
unusual the change was and cannot identify a causal stress effect.
