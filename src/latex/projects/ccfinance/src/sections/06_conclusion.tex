\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

An archived article can enter a publisher's monthly news count as soon
as its source, date and status as a distinct copy are established.
For a bank network, the same article contributes a relationship only
when it names two identified banks, and the treatment of syndicated
copies determines how much weight that relationship receives. An
overnight portfolio requires a further judgment about what was
observable before the cutoff and enough eligible securities in the
same session to form a leg. Recording these decisions alongside an
estimate allows a researcher to see both how much coverage a stricter
rule costs and which firms or dates account for the difference.

CCFinance preserves this record for a frozen selection and provides
audits of issuer identity, subject focus and supporting metadata.
Although the upstream archives and their documentation are publicly
accessible under their respective terms, anonymous requests to the
staging snapshot required authentication, and access to the project
repository and frozen outputs is restricted during review. Once the
required inputs are restored, the recipes can regenerate the reported
analyses (Appendix Table~\ref{tab:app-access}). We plan to release the
code and eligible derived records after review, with terms specified
separately for each artifact. For researchers constructing a new panel,
the ledger makes it possible to trace a change in firm coverage or
portfolio support back to the individual stories that qualified.
