\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The financial quantity of interest determines which eligibility
decisions belong alongside an estimate. For a publisher-month index,
the denominator establishes how much news the publisher contributes.
For a bank network, the article--issuer links and copy rules determine
which relationships are observed and their weights. For a firm-session
portfolio, the capture clock and the number of eligible securities
determine which legs can be evaluated. Because a tighter rule can
change both the number of observations and which firms or dates enter,
the corresponding eligibility ledger is part of the result.

CCFinance preserves those decisions from a frozen source selection and
provides audits of identity, focus and supporting metadata. The upstream
archives and their documentation are publicly accessible under their
respective terms. Access to the project repository and frozen processed
artifacts is restricted. Anonymous requests to the staging snapshot
also required authentication. Once the required inputs are restored, the
recipes can regenerate the reported analysis (Appendix
Table~\ref{tab:app-access}). For a new study, the same record of selection
decisions lets the researcher assess what a more targeted measurement
costs in firms, dates or portfolio support.
