\section{Corpus lineage and source pilot}

The historical filter selects English Fine News records from revision
\texttt{7a841090e69f3c58a9e04}\allowbreak\texttt{b0c39213d879af68e8a}.
It reads the normalized hostname from metadata and falls back to the
URL when that field is blank.
A host qualifies if it exactly matches one of the 115 registry entries or
is a dot-delimited subdomain of one. The frozen downstream input is the
staging revision
\texttt{3168f32f57107eac95a2}\allowbreak\texttt{c53f024e5e340c91ef2e}, with
14,807,287 distinct IDs in 984 row groups across 168 capture-month
shards. Each shard's size and SHA-256 are recorded in the restoration
manifest. The frozen stage verifies that snapshot. A fresh execution of
the historical query writes a consolidated file, and its selected rows
have yet to be compared with the snapshot row for row.

The retrospective pilot expands sampled rows within each of four pinned
release frames (Table~\ref{tab:app-source}). A cluster draw selects a
file and then a row group.
The product of those two probabilities determines the inverse-probability
expansion of its counts. The ratio of the resulting Hansen--Hurwitz
totals gives weighted publisher yield, with exploratory 95\% linearized
$t$ intervals on three degrees of freedom. The manifests preserve the
release versions, draw probabilities and observed counts. Each frame
has its own upstream filtering and deduplication, so the comparison
concerns physical rows. Estimating unique stories, overlap or financial
coverage outside the registry would require separate sampling.

\section{Extractor and metadata audits}

\paragraph{Exact company spans.}
The microbenchmark takes the eight lowest seeded ID hashes per
publication year from 2019 through 2023 among consistently dated,
canonical English articles in the staging snapshot. A lead contains the
first 150 whitespace-delimited words or the entire shorter body. A
GPT-6.2-sol Codex agent at medium reasoning effort annotated all 40
leads, and a separate GPT-6.2-sol agent at low effort annotated the first
two ranks in each year. Both worked blind to extractor predictions.
The repeat agent also worked blind to the primary annotations. The
passes agreed on all ten repeated leads and all 26 reference spans in
them. The frozen primary model reference contains 118 company spans
across 40 leads, eight with none. The guide includes named businesses
and excludes products, bare tickers and nonbusiness organizations.

Stanza 1.11.0 uses the English \texttt{ontonotes\_charlm} model with
combined tokenization and multiword-token expansion. GLiNER2.5 Small
uses the deployed company schema and cutoff 0.5 \citep{stanza,gliner25}.
Scoring matches Stanza ORG or GLiNER company spans by article ID and
exact half-open Unicode offsets. A correctly recognized noncompany ORG
therefore counts as a false positive for this task. Both models recover
69 spans (Table~\ref{tab:app-spans}). GLiNER scores 8.43 micro-F1
percentage points higher in this sample, with a paired 95\% interval
of $[-1.76,+18.76]$ over 2,000 passage bootstraps within publication
year that permits either ordering.
Stanford's published 88.8\% OntoNotes and 92.1\% CoNLL03 scores are
all-class micro-F1 on their benchmark splits \citep{stanza}. In the
local task, a possessive attached to the Credit Suisse Group AG span
cost Stanza its pair with UBS Group AG, while GLiNER missed BofA in a
lead with five stocks and lost five of 15 possible pairs. These are
mention pairs before dated issuer resolution.

\paragraph{Article, mention and window metadata.}
The seed-46 confirmation excludes seed-45 development articles and
samples 400 units from each usable-corpus frame. For form and sentiment,
half the allocation follows predicted class size and half balances
classes subject to availability. Events split equally between windows
with all scores below 0.5 and positive windows stratified by top family.
The GPT-6 Luna low-effort judge sees the article, marked mention in
local context or event window, with predictions withheld. A second Luna
call and a GPT-6.1 Terra low-effort call independently label the same
100 random units per frame. Estimates use inverse-inclusion weights
and article-cluster multiplier intervals holding stratum totals fixed
(Table~\ref{tab:app-metadata}).

The press-release discrepancy persists using usable article--issuer links
($-37.3$ points, 95\% interval $[-47.0,-27.6]$). Counting three
insufficient-context articles as releases would raise the weighted
reference prevalence from 4.18\% to 4.73\%. Sentiment labels have opposite
positive and negative polarity on 7.4\% of weighted extracted mentions.
Weighted sentiment agreement is 82.3\% on the Luna repeat and 57.2\%
between Luna and Terra. The primary judge found at least one event
family in 160 of 200 sampled windows with all predictions below threshold.
The separate 10,000-article extraction benchmark ran at 106.1 articles
per second with 7.22 GiB peak reserved CUDA memory. Synthetic examples
guided development and contribute zero weight to confirmation estimates.

\section{Issuer identity, focus and coverage}

The seed-43 confirmation samples extracted spans by alias frequency
and the number of issuers sharing a key, and draws a separate uniform
sample of articles containing at least one extracted company span.
Article judges receive up to 12,000 body characters. Both draws are
independent of linker predictions. Model
judges receive marked text and neutral issuer search with the candidate
link withheld. A correct link names the judged issuer or the same listed
security on the article date. Weighted intervals are linearized. Unlinked
precision is the share of abstentions judged to have no eligible issuer
(Table~\ref{tab:app-links}).
Cohen's $\kappa$ is 0.935 for the repeated judge on 164 mentions and
0.881 for the independent audit judge on 339.

The anchor audit samples explicit listing anchors outside the seed-43
articles. The decorated-name audit samples newly accepted article--issuer
pairs after normalizing ticker and stock forms. Of 300 newly accepted
pairs, 297 had a trailing ticker and three used the word stock. Six
exchange-prefix cases were checked separately. Normalization adds
49,852 accepted pairs, including 47,808 on usable articles. Agreement
on identity leaves the subject role to be evaluated separately. Short
keys with noncompany meanings and subsidiaries with competing issuer
candidates can produce errors or abstentions. Dated records constrain
valid links, while full-corpus alias priors can use anchors observed later.

\section{Body embeddings and issuer conditioning}

The production EmbeddingGemma artifact \citep{embeddinggemma2025} stores
768 FP16 coordinates per body with a 2,048-token limit. The census
reconciled all IDs, positions and text flags and checked finite values
and norms. All 14,807,287 frozen bodies contained text. A blank-body
fixture exercises the zero-vector convention. A local DVC checkout
restored 985 files including the writer lock, and their hashes matched
manifest \path{68fad30a7cff964595d800d7a7175ff0.dir}.

In the separate issuer-conditioning pilot, a story is eligible when
two companies have distinguishable accounts under the model reference.
A paired success requires both company-conditioned views to prefer
their own name-masked account. Luna identified 18 eligible stories
among 150 held out, of which 16 fit every encoder. Terra found eight
eligible stories in the audit. The primary and audit denominators fell
below their planned minima of 60 and 20. The observed gains from
selecting issuer sentences motivate confirmation on a larger sample
(Table~\ref{tab:app-vectors}).

\paragraph{Equity market volatility.}
The July 2025 manuscript and archive v1 supply the reference tracker
and specifications \citep{baker2025ms,baker2025archive}. Six archive
reruns recover the monthly fits at displayed precision. The substituted
tracker uses dictionary-hit shares in 196 publisher-month cells.
Divide each publisher's shares by its dispersion over the 40
calibration months through December 2022, average across publishers
and rescale to the calibration mean VIX. The comparative fit uses an
intercept and a contemporaneous tracker against each common outcome.
The paired intervals use 10,000 bootstrap samples formed by drawing
circular blocks with replacement,
using the same starts for both trackers before recomputing the
difference in squared correlations. On 49 months, realized-volatility
$R^2$ is 0.336 for CCFinance and 0.569 for the archived tracker. The
gap of $-0.233$ has a six-month block interval of $[-0.311,+0.041]$.

The publisher-omission refits keep dispersion calibration fixed
when excluding the March and April 2020 outcomes. Each three-publisher
tracker retains 147 cells over 49 months and 141 over 47 months.
Omitting Forbes gives the largest shortfalls for both outcomes on
both calendars. Requiring 15 publication dates drops three sparse
cells and yields a VIX gap of $-0.142$ on 49 months. Moving the
calibration endpoint to December 2021 gives a VIX gap of $-0.167$.

observed series.

\paragraph{Overnight portfolios.}
The fixed panel operationalizes a selected design from arXiv v6 and
reference archive v2 \citep{lopez2025v6,lopez2026archive}, using captured
bodies and frozen Yahoo chart prices in place of proprietary headlines
and CRSP. The clock parser recovered 11,196 first-publication markers.

after cutoff. Among the remaining 8,184 articles, 5,392 had no fixed-roster
link. The focus rule rejected 2,510 on-roster links. Of the 995 links
remaining, five failed a within-session similarity screen, one repeated
a recent title and one failed the price-movement headline screen. The
988 survivors passed dated listing, vector and price checks and add
943 unique bodies and 667 firm-sessions. The same 20 of 32 rolling
windows fit, though the recovered panel changes training and evaluation
rows.

\section{Access and reproduction}

Common Crawl makes its captures available and preserves third-party
rights \citep{commoncrawlterms}. Infini-News documents CC BY 4.0 for
derived metadata and an academic research use condition for bulk text
\citep{lazzaroni2026}. Fine News identifies AGPL-3.0-only for its code
and assigns no common license to underlying stories \citep{solovev2026}.
CCFinance's Apache-2.0 license applies to the project code. The Baker
and L\'opez-Lira--Tang archives are listed under CC BY 4.0
\citep{baker2025archive,lopez2026archive}. Proprietary inputs such as
Factiva, RavenPack and CRSP have separate access requirements.

Exact reconstruction requires the authorized project revision, the
locked environment and matching frozen inputs. Code and the project
artifact store are private. On 22 November 2026, anonymous requests
for the exact staging revision
and for bytes of a staged shard returned HTTP 401. The pinned upstream
Fine News revision returned HTTP 200. Public upstream archives and
reference packages supply only some dependencies. Run the commands in
Table~\ref{tab:app-access} from the repository root in the pinned
environment after restoring the corresponding dependencies and verifying
their revisions or DVC checksums. Frozen reports read saved labels or
predictions where available. Rebuilding extraction requires the
configured runtime and model cache, and new adjudication requires access
to the relevant models. Codex assisted with outlining, drafting,
editing, and consistency checks.
