A story about one firm's earnings may also name its competitors, so a
researcher measuring firm coverage must decide whether to count the
story for every firm it mentions or only for those it concerns. For a frozen
Common Crawl News sample, CCFinance selects canonical copies, checks
publication dates against capture, and links company mentions to issuers
on the article date. Of 34,781 issuer-years with listing evidence
from 2017 through
2025, 13,918 have at least 50 usable linked articles. Requiring three
mentions per article reduces that count to 8,641 and, among held-out
articles with a detected company, raises subject precision while lowering
recall. On a common calendar the archived-news volatility tracker
explains less variation than the reference tracker, although the paired
interval for the difference spans zero. From January to April 2020,
bank-pair counts rose across publishers but fell within Reuters.
Recovering publication times increases the number of overnight sessions
with both portfolio legs from 42 to 110, while mean open-to-close drift
remains negative after refitting. Exact reconstruction requires access
to the frozen inputs and derived artifacts.
