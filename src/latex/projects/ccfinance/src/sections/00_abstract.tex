Rules for turning archived news into financial observations change
which firms, dates and estimates a study can support. We construct
CCFinance from a frozen selection of Common Crawl News and trace
article versions through publication-date checks, deduplication and
dated issuer linking. Among 100 reference firms, 94 have at least 47
usable linked articles in each year from 2018 through 2022. Requiring
three mentions of the issuer reduces that count to 57. In a held-out
model-adjudicated audit, the rule raises subject precision and lowers
subject recall. On a shared calendar, a volatility tracker built from
archived articles has lower point-estimate fit than the reference
tracker, with a paired interval spanning zero for the difference.
The January--April 2020 change in distinct co-mentioned bank pairs
reverses sign in the Reuters subset. Recovering publication clocks
raises the number of sessions with both portfolio legs from 42 to
110, and mean open-to-close drift stays negative after refitting.
Exact reconstruction requires access to the frozen inputs and
derived artifacts.
