Reconstructing a financial news sample requires the observed article
version and the rules that determine which observations it supplies.
CCFinance records these decisions for a frozen Common Crawl News
selection through publication-date checks, deduplication and dated
issuer linking. Among 100 reference firms, 94 have at least 47 usable
linked articles in each year from 2018 through 2022. Requiring three
mentions of the issuer in each counted article reduces that number
to 57. The rule raises subject precision and lowers subject recall
in a held-out model-adjudicated audit. On a common calendar, the
archived-news volatility tracker has lower estimated fit than the
reference tracker, with a paired interval for the difference spanning
zero. The January--April 2020 change in distinct co-mentioned bank
pairs reverses sign in the Reuters subset. Recovering publication
clocks raises the number of sessions with both portfolio legs from
42 to 110, while mean open-to-close drift remains negative after
refitting. Exact reconstruction requires access to the frozen inputs
and derived artifacts.
